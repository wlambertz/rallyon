## Findings

1. **Event and provisional applicability:** Jugendspielordnung § 7 identifies DBV as organizer of the U11 ranking tournaments. Anlage I § 11(1) specifies preliminary groups of four followed by a knockout main round for A-RLT U11 singles and permits adjustments to the tournament format. Anlage I § 30 is in its tournament-conduct section and provides group rankings. The note correctly limits itself to the stated edition and recommends confirming the actual event announcement and valid changes; no current RallyOn event or product target is proved.
2. **Ranking and incomplete-group exception:** Anlage I § 30(1) lists match wins, set difference and point difference. Section 30(2) removes all games of a participant unable to complete their group games from the group calculation; it then states a direct-comparison exception for two players with equal wins in a group where not all matches could be played. The note captures that conditional exception; it does not claim that the direct-comparison outcome exists in every incomplete group.
3. **Examples and corrections:** The completed-group example follows § 30(1), conditional on A and B being tied on match wins and A having the better set difference. The withdrawal example correctly says exclusion from the ranking calculation, not deletion of historical results. The missing-result and correction examples identify unresolved or proposed system behavior; the source sections reviewed do not themselves define application correction permissions, audit storage or a general failure-state contract. These examples do not by themselves constitute implementation-ready acceptance criteria or independent proof of every edge case.

The note correctly avoids silently applying or replacing one provision with the other; making U11 the product target or changing approved requirements needs the applicable lifecycle review.
