## Independent findings

1. The [DBV tournament calendar](https://turniere.badminton.de/) lists German Masters U11 in Hofheim on 5–6 December 2026, in singles and doubles. The DBV [2026 event allocation](https://www.badminton.de/news/badminton/turniervergabe-nachwuchsveranstaltungen-2026/) lists a U11 Masters Finale on those dates. These are supporting event listings, not the event's actual announcement or proof of its eventual organization or play.
2. [Jugendspielordnung Anlage I §§ 11 and 13](https://www.badminton.de/fileadmin/dateien_upload/dateien_2026/juli_2026/dbv_satzungswerk_2026-27_stand_13.06.2026.pdf), PDF pages 295–296, distinguish the four-person U11 singles group format at A-RLT from the U11 Masters' three-person preliminary groups in singles and doubles. The investigation states the distinction accurately. The general regulation permits mode changes for A-RLT; the event-specific document and any valid modifications were not established in this review.
3. The same PDF, Jugendspielordnung Anlage I § 30(1)–(2), PDF page 301, gives the stated order of wins, set difference and point difference, and the exclusion from the ranking calculation if an entrant cannot complete group games. It gives the special head-to-head rule in the stated incomplete-group, equal-wins condition. Neither the research note nor this review assumes that excluding a result from a calculation means deleting a historical result.
4. The completed three-entrant cycle example gives each entrant one win. If C's games are excluded and the one remaining A–B match is completed and decisive, its winner has one win and its loser zero: the research note correctly avoids presenting them as tied. Unplayed matches or other arrangements need separate, explicitly specified examples; this arithmetic does not prove the official rule's treatment of every residual case.
5. The approved G2 scenario explicitly names the distinct Spielordnung Anlage IV Teil 2 § 10 pool provision. The investigation correctly does not equate that provision with the Jugendspielordnung § 30, and treats correction/retention and architecture ownership as proposed or unresolved.
