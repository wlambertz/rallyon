# Proposed dispositions for issue 183

These are analysis recommendations, not approval to implement. The detailed IDs, evidence and authority limitations are in `inventory.md` and `analysis.md`. Persona values are hypotheses (Organizer / Player / Spectator); effort is a coarse future-delivery estimate, not a commitment. The present task remains analysis only.

## Confirmed narrow gaps versus skipped candidates

Direct inspection of `ConfigurationServiceImpl.kt` confirms that its `publish`, `openRegistration`, `closeRegistration` and `lockConfiguration` methods throw `UnsupportedOperationException`. These are confirmed gaps in those specific service operations, not proof that every possible publication or entry workflow is absent. The prospectus row below gives publication an initial high/medium/medium persona assessment and medium effort; the entry row gives registration high/high/low and high effort. Their exact scope, policy and work decomposition still need refinement. No additional follow-up for these gaps has been accepted or created.

The remaining broad N-ID clusters below are recommendations for research and prioritization of provisional or explicitly skipped claims. They are not a list of independently confirmed implementation gaps. Each skipped source statement retains its own reason in the `trace-sections-*` files. This distinction is important when judging which gaps meet #183's follow-up criterion.
