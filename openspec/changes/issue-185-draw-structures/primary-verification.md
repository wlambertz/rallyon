## P3: concrete case and inference

The announcement uses doubles/singles/mixed, double knockout with consolation, possible format changes according to entry count, and DBV ranking at draw time. It allows draw adjustment for seeded withdrawals or format changes until shortly before the event (pp. 1–2). These event-specific instructions support investigating a deliberate participant-field decision before drawing. They do not justify selecting pure knockout or importing international automatic seed movement. HBV provisions and actual event execution were not independently checked; no claim of software compliance follows.

## Explicitly unverified / deferred

The checklist's clothing, medical, call-timing, equipment, advertising, monetary penalties and ITTO/Law assertions remain unverified here and are assigned to #184/#186. GCR technical diagrams, exact World Senior group-to-KO placement, and event-specific HBV overrides remain unverified. No such claim enters a candidate obligation. Mathematical example: for 17 units, 32 slots minus 17 is 15 byes; with each unit appearing once and no bye-v-bye pairing, the first round has one played match. This arithmetic does not determine placement policy.
