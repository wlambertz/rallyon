# Match-day guide claim inventory

This inventory records what the [supplied source](../../../docs/wiki/References/Badminton-Regel-Kompendium.md) says, not what RallyOn or an authority has accepted. IDs use its section and table row, numbered item, bullet, or checklist position. Semicolon-separated propositions in a row share the ID and are not necessarily verified together. The authority column is the source's attribution, not proof of applicability. `S` means potentially software-relevant, `I` information, `D` international sanction deferred. Unless explicitly qualified, **unverified** means not established from the cited applicable primary edition; it does not mean false. See [findings](findings.md) for primary-source comparisons and reconciliation. An identified competition and rule edition remain prerequisites for applying any rule.
