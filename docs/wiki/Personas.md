# Personas

> **Draft.** These personas only restate the three audiences named on [Home](Home.md). The wiki's `Personas.md` was empty from its first commit, so there is no earlier content to restore. Refine them with real goals, pain points, and tournament context before relying on them for UX decisions.

## Organizer

Plans and runs a badminton tournament (Kaiserturnier, Swiss system, or knockout).

- Wants automated match schedules, clear hall allocation, and integrated result entry.
- Success: less manual effort and a smooth tournament day.

## Player

Takes part in a tournament.

- Wants current results, upcoming matches, and their personal tournament history in real time.
- Success: always knows where to be and when, and trusts the process to be fair and transparent.

## Spectator

Follows a tournament live in the hall or online.

- Wants results, interim scores, and rankings on demand, clearly presented and current.
- Success: can follow the action without asking anyone.
