# Independent value challenge at G3

Give a fresh checker the approved scope/specification, value assessment and `docs/value-scoring.md`. Require them to reproduce the calculation and inspect supporting raw evidence. Do not supply the maker's desired verdict.

Check user, learning and risk scores against the documented anchors, confidence against evidence, and effort across implementation, tests, documentation and delivery. Identify unsupported precision, ignored dependencies and assumptions presented as facts. Record corrections, unresolved questions and pass/fail with the checker identity.

The model is advisory, with no automatic approval threshold. A material change to value, effort, confidence, scope or architecture returns to the human G2 decision. Reduced mode may omit the numerical assessment with a concrete reason in `lifecycle.md`; independent readiness checking remains required.
