# Persona refinement at G1

Read `docs/wiki/Personas.md` and the context map. Identify the primary audience, owning app/context and one concrete problem. Device contexts and candidate metrics in the personas remain hypotheses; distinguish observed evidence, maintainer statements and assumptions.

Ask only questions that materially affect scope, success or delivery. Explore code/docs for discoverable facts first. Challenge the need with the primary persona's actual constraints: organizer interruptions and hall connectivity, player glanceability and timing, or spectator information freshness. For developer-workflow changes, state the maintainer benefit and the indirect product benefit rather than inventing a product persona task.

Record the current behavior, desired outcome, exclusions, measurable or observable success criteria, unresolved assumptions, compatibility risks and owning context. Record a work budget if agreed; do not invent one. Stop with a concrete scope artifact for human approval before specification work advances.
